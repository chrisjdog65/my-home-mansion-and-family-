The Property

A four-level mountain-view estate. The ground floor holds the great room with its double-height stone fireplace, chef's kitchen, dining hall, library, two family rooms and the indoor pool wing with a hot tub. Upstairs are twenty-two bedrooms including the master suite, James's and Chloie's rooms, and two dedicated gaming rooms with high-end PCs. The basement hides the movie theater auditorium, a two-lane bowling alley, a full basketball court, the gym, the laundry and the workshop.

The Grounds

Terraced lawn, a stone-edged pond fed by a running waterfall, a covered picnic area, a concrete skate bowl with a quarter pipe and rails, a fully stocked shed, and a driveway with a supercharged Ford pickup and a Lamborghini.
